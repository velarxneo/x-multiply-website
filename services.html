SERVICES

Consulting support built for F&B operations

Every engagement starts with a conversation about where your business is today and what you're trying to solve. From there, we scope the support to fit.

1

Business & Operations Advisory

We review how your outlet or business currently operates — from daily workflows to how decisions get made — and work with you to identify where things can run more smoothly.

2

Growth & Expansion Planning

Thinking about a second outlet, a new concept, or entering a new market? We help structure the planning, timeline, and decisions involved in getting there.

3

Cost & Menu Strategy

General advisory on menu structure, portioning, and pricing discipline aimed at supporting healthier, more predictable margins.

4

Process & SOP Guidance

Support in documenting clearer standard operating procedures, so your business can run consistently across shifts and outlets.

5

Manpower & Training Advisory

Advisory input on team structure, role clarity, and training frameworks to help build a more capable, consistent front and back of house.

6

Ad-hoc Business Advisory

Not every question fits neatly into a category. If you have a specific business challenge, we're happy to start with a conversation.
